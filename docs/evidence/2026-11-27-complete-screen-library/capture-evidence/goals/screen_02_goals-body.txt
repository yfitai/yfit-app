Understanding Your Body Type

Your body type helps us personalise your training and nutrition recommendations.

Ectomorph

Naturally lean, fast metabolism

• Slim frame, narrow shoulders
• Fast metabolism, hard to gain weight
• Low body fat naturally
• Long limbs relative to torso

Focus: Calorie surplus, compound lifts, adequate rest

Mesomorph

Athletic build, responds well to training

• Naturally muscular and athletic
• Gains muscle and loses fat easily
• Medium frame, broad shoulders
• Responds quickly to exercise

Focus: Balanced training, moderate calories, variety

Endomorph

Stocky build, slower metabolism

• Wider frame, higher body fat tendency
• Slower metabolism, gains weight easily
• Strong lower body
• Good strength potential

Focus: Calorie deficit, cardio + strength, consistency

Note: Most people are a combination of two body types. Your results reflect your dominant type.
